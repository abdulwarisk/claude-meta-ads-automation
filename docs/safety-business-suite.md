# Extra tip: stop Claude from raising your budget

Meta lets you control what AI connectors can do on each ad account.

1. Open **Meta Business Suite → Settings → Integrations → Ads MCP server**.
2. Choose the ad account.
3. Leave on what you want Claude to do (for example creating campaigns and ads).
4. **Block "edit budgets".**

Now Claude can build campaigns for you, but it can't increase spend by itself.
Menu names may change while the product is in beta. If you can't find it, search Business Suite help for "Ads MCP server".
